# Drone Pocket Logbook: Privacy Policy

_Last updated October 19, 2026_

Drone Pocket Logbook does not collect, transmit or sell any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

- **On your iPhone only.** Your pilot profile, drones, flights and documents are stored on your device. Document files and your profile use iOS complete file protection (unreadable while your iPhone is locked).
- **IACRA login and FTN** are stored only in your iPhone's Keychain, marked this-device-only and never synced to iCloud. They are shown only after Face ID or your passcode and are never included in exports.
- **Location** is used only when you tap "Use Current Location" on a flight, and only the coordinates you save are kept.
- **Notifications** are local reminders scheduled on your device.
- **Purchases** are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details.
- **Exports** (CSV/PDF) are created only when you choose to share them.

Deleting the app deletes its data. Questions: contact the developer through the App Store listing.
